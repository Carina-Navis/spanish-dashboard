Areas to Review

✓ Si (if) vs Sí (yes) — RESOLVED Oct 14 (6/6)
✓ como si + imperfect = present unreality · como si + pluperfect = past unreality — RESOLVED Oct 14
⚠ si + pluperfect subj → conditional PERFECT (habría ganado), NOT simple conditional (ganaría)
✓ si + present → FUTURE result (perderás), NOT present (pierdes) — BREAKTHROUGH Oct 27 ✓
⚠ Reported speech: en ese momento (never drop the en); para mí not para me after prepositions
⚠ Reported speech person shifts: tenemos→tenían, me siento→se sentía; verse vs venir
⚠ Adverbial conjunctions = ALWAYS subjunctive; present main→present subj, past main→imperfect subj
⚠ el hecho de que → ALWAYS subjunctive even for real/known facts (Oct 28 assessment: wrote indicative)
⚠ a pesar de + NOUN (la lluvia), NOT conjugated verb (llueva); OR: a pesar de que + subj
⚠ Ser for events/locations: la reunión ES en... (never está); ser ≠ temporary here
⚠ aunque + subj → main clause STAYS INDICATIVE: "aunque llegues, no tengo tiempo" (Oct 29: wrote no tenga)
